Numbers you can trust

  • A readiness reading with no baseline can be Vigilar at most, never Acción, and says so in one sentence everywhere: «38 hoy · aún sin su base (0 de 7 lecturas)».
  • «Proponer descarga» reads the signal that asked for it; when its engine says «keep», the row says why instead of claiming a deload was proposed.
  • One vocabulary (Acción / Vigilar / Al día), one definition of «Sin plan», «Empieza pronto» separate, one source for where an athlete is in a program — Hoy, Atletas, groups and the athlete page now agree.
  • «Todo» on Hoy includes the athletes waiting for a reply; the weekly verdict and the daily notifications come from the same signals as Hoy (a test builds one athlete-week and checks they agree).
  • Load numbers (CTL/ACWR/TSB) are hidden until there are 42 days of history; Fisiología reads the real check-ins.

Programming

  • «r2» in the quick line is 2 minutes; 11–14 asks; the unit is always shown in bold.
  • A new club's catalogue now contains the main lifts — in production they had been inserted by hand, so every fresh database (a new FLEXR tenant included) started with 71 mobility exercises and no squat (0247).
  • Groups say plainly that each athlete continues at their own week, and assigning offers «everyone starts Monday».

Platform

  • A fresh database now migrates from zero in one run: all 238 migrations, checked on an empty Postgres 16. The only thing that blocked it was the migration runner itself, which sent 0051's three CREATE INDEX CONCURRENTLY as one query; it now sends them one by one. The «tenant #1 data in old migrations» feared in the first review did not break anything.

Your decisions

These are not correctness problems; each is a business or product choice with real consequences. For each one I give the options and what I would do, but the call is yours. Nothing below is built until you decide.

1 · Which app do other clubs' athletes install?

  1. One FLEXR app; clubs are skins inside it — new App Store listing, FAHYBRID's athletes reinstall once.
  2. The FAHYBRID app for everyone — nothing to build; club #2's athletes install a competitor's brand.
  3. One shared app plus white-label builds per club as a premium tier — Apple's template-app rule (4.2.6) applies: each build must ship from the client's own developer account, and push, Sign in with Apple, universal links and watch workouts need per-build work.

I'd pick 1, before coach #2 — it matches the decision already written (FLEXR as the umbrella, 2026-08-19) and keeps option 3 open for later.

2 · Who takes the athletes' money?

  1. Our platform account, attributed per club — we hold other clubs' money; accounting and tax per club are ours.
  2. Stripe Connect — each club has its own account, we take a platform fee; most work, cleanest legally.
  3. Clubs bill outside the app; Cobros only shows status — fastest, no payment revenue.

I'd pick 2; if speed matters, launch coach #2 on 3 and move to Connect. Until decided, paid sign-up must stay off for any club but FAHYBRID — today it charges FAHYBRID's Stripe.

3 · How does a coach get in?

  1. Open self-serve sign-up with a trial.
  2. Request plus our approval — the allowlist exists; the step that creates the club does not.
  3. Invite-only, created by us.

I'd pick 2 for the first 20 coaches: quality control while the product is young, and the smallest amount of new sign-up code.

4 · What does FLEXR charge a coach?

  1. A flat plan plus add-ons (Negocio, the assistant connector).
  2. Per active athlete.
  3. Free core, pay for add-ons only.

Pricing is yours. Mechanically, 1 fits what exists (add-ons are already entitlements). Nothing bills a coach today in any option.

5 · One deployment for everyone, or one per club?

  1. One multi-tenant deployment — today's direction; background jobs must be split per coach before ~20 coaches.
  2. A deployment and database per club — strongest isolation, N× operations, and a fresh database migrates cleanly from zero (fixed after the review).

I'd pick 1, plus decision 10 for defence in depth.

6 · Domains

  1. One panel domain for all clubs, public funnels at /c/<club>.
  2. A subdomain per club — tenant-by-host must be built.
  3. Custom domain per club.

I'd pick 1; 3 can be a premium extra later. Also yours: does fahybrid.com stay FAHYBRID's own site, and where does FLEXR's marketing live?

7 · Which sports?

  1. HYROX / hybrid only — the default tests, race catalogue and methodology groups are right as they are.
  2. Any coach — those become optional starter packs.

I'd pick 1 at launch. Because the method is now the coach's data, 2 stays possible without a redesign.

8 · Language of the panel

  1. Spanish only at launch.
  2. English before the first non-Spanish coach — about 420 files to translate.

Depends on your first market. Spain and Latin America first → 1; anyone else → start the translation now.

9 · What does a new club start with?

  1. Empty, with the setup checklist.
  2. A neutral starter pack we write (levels N1–N5 already exist as a one-click start).
  3. A copy of FAHYBRID's content — needs Pablo's consent, and it is tenant #1 leaking in as «defaults».

I'd pick 2.

10 · Database-level isolation (row-level security)

Today every query must remember to filter by coach; the three holes above were exactly that failure. Postgres can enforce it instead: a policy per table, and the app sets the coach once per request.

  1. Full row-level security — about 40 tables of policies, 2–4 days threading the request's coach through the loaders, slightly slower queries.
  2. Cheaper first step — a check in CI that flags queries on club tables without a coach filter, plus the two-coach test pattern on every route with an id.

I'd do 2 now and 1 before coach #20.

11 · Legal and the landing

Terms and privacy name Pablo (Fabrik) as the data controller for every athlete. For FLEXR each club must be the controller and FLEXR the processor, with a data-processing agreement. The public pages (booking, invite, payment, unsubscribe) still say FAHYBRID; what they should say follows from decision 1. This needs a lawyer, not code.

12 · Permission to delete dead files

The permission system blocks agents from deleting files, and I won't do it on their behalf. About 20 files have no importers (listed in FOCUS.md); they are excluded from lint meanwhile. One «yes» and they go.
