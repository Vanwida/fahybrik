FAHYBRID · athlete app · iPhone + Apple Watch · 24 Sept 2026

Why the athlete app keeps breaking — and the order to fix it

Six reviewers read the whole iPhone and Watch app (717 Swift files) and the server it talks to. The engine inside is well built. What breaks is around it: the Watch link is designed so it can fail, a finished workout isn't safe until the server accepts it, what the coach changes often doesn't reach the athlete, and the app says things no coach decided. Under all of it, nothing compiles or tests the iOS code before you do.

How this was checked. This environment has no Xcode, simulator, iPhone or Watch, so nothing in the app was compiled or run: every finding was traced line by line in the Swift and server code, and each is marked confirmed (traced) or suspected (needs your device). «El doble» was used where it's current, but 8 of its 9 app mirrors are stale. Anything only a device can prove is in the tests to run. Branch claude/focused-bardeen-u9zz33; full reports in the session scratchpad.

Your two complaints, answered first

«The Watch sometimes connects, sometimes not»

It isn't Bluetooth. Four design causes, all in the code:

  • The workout clock lives on the iPhone, and iOS may suspend the iPhone when it locks. The app never declares the background mode for workouts; it stays alive only by accident (GPS on outdoor runs, a silent audio engine on timed formats, nothing at all on strength days). The Watch then freezes on the last screen.
  • One launch attempt. The Watch is asked to join once, at ▶ EMPEZAR. If the Watch is asleep, locked or slow, the workout runs without it and nothing says so. There's no «Reintentar» on the phone and no «Unirse» on the Watch.
  • Messages without a name. The phone sends «workout ended» to the Watch after every session, even ones the Watch never joined, and that message carries no workout id and never expires. It can arrive when the next workout starts and end it — or discard it.
  • Two ways to start on the wrist. The «Apple Watch» setting sends runs to Apple's own Workout app, which never mirrors to the phone. Our Watch app does. Same run, two paths, only one «connects».

And the phone shows «Apple Watch» as connected whenever the Watch app is merely installed, so you can't tell which of these happened.

«Too many issues»

The root is the process, not any one bug:

  • No compiler in the loop. The Swift has been written by agents on Linux, where it can't be compiled. At least 8 of builds 86–98 failed to compile in Xcode Cloud, and 12 of the last 83 iOS commits exist only to fix compile or archive failures.
  • Tests never run on their own. There are 1,884 unit tests, but they only run when someone runs them on a Mac. The one UI test skips itself. Several tests only grep the source text.
  • No eyes on production. No crash reporting, no hang reports, no record of failed saves or Watch-link failures. Every problem is found by an athlete, one at a time.

So the first fix is not a bug fix: it's a Mac that compiles and tests every iOS change before it merges. Without that, fixing the bugs below repeats the cause.

Six root problems

Root 1

The Watch link is built so it can fail, and it can't say what happened

The direction of the last rewrite (FH-56) is right and stays: only Apple's own events change the link state, no home-made watchdogs, never throw away the athlete's recording. It was the seventh rewrite in 14 days and has never been run on a device. It still lacks the three things Apple leaves to the app: keep the coach alive, let either side (re)join, and name every message with its workout.

Root 4

The app says things no coach decided

The panel was just made honest: no «critical» without a baseline, adherence only on sessions that were due, no load numbers without 42 days of history. The app contradicts it, and it breaks Hard Rule Nº0 (the method is the coach's, not the code's):

  • P0 After a single reading, a red readiness ring and «Hoy toca aflojar: recorta volumen», while the coach sees «38 ayer · aún sin su base (1 de 7 lecturas)». In the local data, 14 athletes would get that advice on a day with a visible session.
  • Training advice written by nobody: «Aprieta menos esta semana», «Afina y descansa» from 21 days before a race, a daily «Haz una simulación de HYROX».
  • One coach's method stated as fact: a coach «pausing you while you recover» (even when the athlete paused themself), microcycles and CTL/ATL/TSB explanations in Perfil, N1–N4 self-levelling, a fixed HYROX questionnaire, a jump test fixed at 15 kg and 3 attempts.
Root 5

Starting and ending a workout is harder than the market standard

  • The home doesn't say what to train today. The app opens on Inicio, whose only orange button is «Crear entreno libre»; today's session lives in the Plan tab. Runna, TrainingPeaks and Garmin put it on the first screen.
  • 5–7 taps to start: brief → devices → the same brief again → ▶ EMPEZAR → ▶ ARRANCAR BLOQUE. Apple Workout, Strava, Runna: 1–3.
  • Ending is hidden behind a 34-pt pause button next to an ✕ labelled «Salir del entreno» that only minimises; on an outdoor run the big button closes the segment.
  • The main session's actions (move, mark done, ask the coach) exist only as a long-press. The end of a rest is a vibration only, with no sound or notification.
  • Legibility: the type tokens themselves are 11–13 pt, under the project's own 15-pt floor; about 1,300 text sites ignore Dynamic Type; the «faint» text colour is 2.8–3.3:1 contrast.
  • Notifications are asked cold right after sign-in, with no way back if refused; the icon badge never clears; a race-reminder push opens nowhere.
Root 6

Nothing compiles, tests or watches the app before athletes do

Covered above under «too many issues». It's listed as a root because it's why the other five exist and keep coming back.

The plan

Ordered so each phase makes the next one safe. Everything from phase 1 on is Swift, so it waits for phase 0.

Phase 0 · A compiler, tests and eyes in the loop

1–3 days · needs your OK for the Mac build minutes
  • A Mac build on every change to ios/: generate the project, compile the iPhone and Watch apps, run the 1,884 tests. A red build can't merge. This also gives me a compiler from here: I push, the Mac compiles, I read the result.
  • Crash and hang reports (Apple's MetricKit, free, no third-party SDK) plus a small log of failed saves and Watch-link events, sent to our server.
  • A hidden «Diagnóstico del reloj» screen that shows the last link events from both devices and lets you send them, so testing the Watch doesn't need a Mac and a cable.

Phase 1 · Never lose the athlete's work, and tell the truth

1–2 weeks · app + the server fixes above
  • A finished workout is saved on the phone at «Terminar», before anything else; the summary edits it; sending is separate. Three honest end states: «Guardado» / «Guardado en el móvil · se enviará solo» / «No se pudo guardar · motivo». Offline workouts never expire; the queue belongs to the athlete who made it.
  • One source for the session token (ends the logout loop), and a session that renews while it's used.
  • Refresh on returning to the app and on a new day; the Watch's «today» carries its date.
  • Readiness shows «calibrando · n de 7» until there's a baseline, uses the coach's bands, and gives no advice.
  • A manual pause never resumes on its own; sets follow the prescription, not the exercise category; the load sheet never overwrites a set.

Phase 2 · The Watch link, as reliable as Apple allows

1–2 weeks after phase 0 · proven on your devices with the tests below before and after
  • Every message carries its workout id; an «ended» only ends its own workout, and old ones are cancelled at every start.
  • The phone declares the workout background mode, and while the Watch records, the Watch's clock is the session clock — no time is thrown away.
  • Either side can join the same workout: «Reintentar» on the phone, «Entreno en curso en el iPhone · Unirse» on the Watch. Re-link when the phone comes back in range.
  • One honest status on both screens (below). Distance sent as totals, not increments. Work started on the Watch is never dropped.

Phase 3 · The workout and the app at market standard

2–4 weeks · design first in «el doble», then build
  • Today's session on the home screen; one pre-workout screen and one «Empezar» that also starts the first block.
  • A clear End control; a big pause at the bottom on runs; ✕ becomes «Minimizar»; 44-pt targets; the 15-pt floor and Dynamic Type everywhere.
  • Timed and distance sets with their own timer and record; load and effort on the same set (80 % + RIR 2); a score per block and per HYROX station; the EMOM button marks the minute done instead of skipping the rest.
  • Rest as a deadline with a sound and a lock-screen Live Activity for every workout, not only outdoor runs.
  • Notifications asked in context with a way back in Perfil; the badge in sync; comunicados shown where the coach anchored them.

Phase 4 · Your call: the Watch as the clock

After phase 2 is proven

Phase 2 makes the link as good as it can be while the clock lives on the iPhone, which iOS is still allowed to suspend. Every app that feels solid on the wrist — Apple Workout, Runna, Nike Run Club, Strava, WorkOutDoors — keeps the clock on the Watch, which is never suspended during a workout, and treats the phone as a second screen. The engine already runs on the Watch for solo workouts, so it's a change of owner, not a new engine. The cost: the phone's richer live screens become followers.

What's good and must stay

  • The engine: one workout engine shared by phone and Watch, one rule for «next», what was prescribed kept separate from what was done, every leg of a structured run recorded, one writer on the server.
  • FH-56's rules for the link: only Apple's events decide, no watchdog acts on a guess, one launch per workout, never discard the athlete's recording.
  • The Watch canvas: one subject per page, the zone as a colour field, Always-On handled once, the bezel ring that draws the structure. Closer to Apple Workout than most third-party apps.
  • Things no competitor has: chat that knows which session you're asking about, structured coach items (protocols, tasks, questions), the «5 de 6 dentro» verdict after a run.
  • Honest data habits: no invented pace or zones, empty states that say what's missing, a cache that opens instantly, the session in the Keychain, Apple Health anchors that never skip data.
  • Brand and version in one place, and the build number visible in both apps.
