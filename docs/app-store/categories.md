# FAHYBRIK — App Store categories

Set in App Store Connect → My Apps → FAHYBRIK → App Information → Category.

## Primary category

**Health & Fitness**

Justification: FAHYBRIK is a training/coaching app with HealthKit integration (HRV, sleep, weight, workouts). Health & Fitness is where serious-athlete training apps live (Whoop, Strong, Hevy, TrainingPeaks). It also lets the app surface in the iOS Health app's "App categories" recommendations.

## Secondary category

**Sports**

Justification: HYROX is a sport. Pablo's athletes are competitors. Sports as a secondary category surfaces FAHYBRIK alongside Strava, Pelo, race apps when athletes browse Sports — a tighter audience match than generic Fitness.

## Categories explicitly considered and rejected

- **Lifestyle** — too broad, dilutes the élite-athlete signal.
- **Medical** — would trigger additional review (FDA-style) requirements we don't need; we don't claim diagnosis.
- **Education** — even though there's an ATR/methodology layer, the primary surface is execution, not learning.

## Subcategory note

Apple does not expose a HYROX or "hybrid training" subcategory; primary "Health & Fitness" is the closest. Don't burn a keyword slot on the word "Sports" in the keywords field — the secondary category covers that signal.
